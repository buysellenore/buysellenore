Writing sample

How I write a finding

Sample
Fictional scenarioArea: Logical accessRating: High
Condition
Of 25 sampled leavers, 4 still held active accounts in the finance application between 9 and 41 days after their termination date.
Criteria
Access policy requires removal within 1 business day of termination. Supporting criteria: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls 5.18 (Access rights) and 6.5 (Responsibilities after termination or change of employment), as applied through the organisation's Statement of Applicability.
Cause
HR termination notices reached IT by email only. No reconciliation was run between the HR leaver list and application accounts.
Effect
Former staff could access financial data after leaving, and the quarterly access review did not detect it.
Rating basis
High: a 16% exception rate (4 of 25) on a financially significant system, with the detective control (the quarterly review) also failing to catch it.
Recommendation
Trigger deprovisioning from the HR system and run a weekly leaver-to-account reconciliation, with exceptions tracked to closure.
